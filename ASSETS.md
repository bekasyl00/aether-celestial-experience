# Asset provenance and notices

## Original procedural collection

The 1,000 angels and demons, their detailed inspection meshes, skeletons, weapons, portals, environments and effects are generated by authored JavaScript in `src/sculptures.js`, `src/rig.js`, `src/realm.js` and `src/combat.js`. These are original procedural designs, not scans or downloaded character packs. The ambient sound is synthesized with Web Audio; no recorded music or third-party samples are bundled.

Version 1 used two generated image backdrops. Version 2 replaces that presentation with a fully volumetric scene; the old images are no longer distributed in this version.

## Museum archive: Engel

- **Author:** noe-3d.at.
- **Original:** [Engel on Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/engel-15edd1e0e422482586dd4dd4aebe8d55), a scanned angel at the Grafenberg cemetery entrance in Austria.
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **Prepared by:** Mehmet Kerem Turkcan for Valdrada. The prepared version removes the pedestal, centers and rescales the mesh, simplifies it into three levels of detail, and uses a grayscale cavity texture.
- **Preparation record:** [Valdrada source](https://github.com/mkturkcan/valdrada/blob/main/client/tools/cp33/bethesda/angel.mjs) and [license table](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mehmetkeremturkcan/valdrada/blob/main/LICENSE.md).
- **Pinned download:** [angel.glb, revision efd0c5b](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mehmetkeremturkcan/valdrada/resolve/efd0c5bb99dae30710614e17fd7b69d1a4d73763/Content/models/cp33/bethesda/angel.glb).
- **Bundled file:** `public/models/angel.glb`, 11,215,596 bytes. SHA-256: `6355bebc9ec8761083d932b120df098074778d2c9b8fa60906fe789a22c8e57c`.
- **AETHER display changes:** scale, placement, lighting and material appearance. Only LOD0 (220,000 triangles) is displayed in the close view; the overlapping lower LODs are hidden. The model is displayed directly as a static sculpture, with no skeletal deformation. It has not been used as an image-generation or training input.

Attribution: “Engel” by noe-3d.at, CC BY 4.0. Prepared by Mehmet Kerem Turkcan for Valdrada; displayed in AETHER with the changes noted above. Neither the original author nor the preparer endorses this project.

## Museum archive: Kneeling winged monster

- **Institution:** Smithsonian Institution, National Museum of Asian Art / Freer Gallery of Art.
- **Object:** *Kneeling winged monster*, F1916.345; limestone, Northern Qi dynasty, 550–577, China.
- **Primary record:** [Smithsonian 3D](https://3d.si.edu/object/3d/kneeling-winged-monster%3A0dc68216-3651-44c7-99cf-18e5d4d1eb9f).
- **License:** [CC0 1.0 Public Domain Dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **GLB distribution:** [Innerscene public model library](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/kneeling-winged-monster-32949d31).
- **Bundled file:** `public/models/guardian.glb`, 10,873,824 bytes, 149,999 triangles. SHA-256: `220d38e1ea848f31dd6fd8ba11552b19da127c4f9c46494f145602c4a69a2864`.
- **Display changes:** normalized scale, placement and lighting. This crouching guardian creature is an archive sculpture, not a rigged humanoid demon.

Both GLBs contain their own textures. The archive loads on demand from this site's own origin. The source-code MIT license does not relicense either model.
